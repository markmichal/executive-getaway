Event summary

What

Executive Getaway is an annual gathering of Cru partners and friends to celebrate what God is doing among high school students, hear from national and international leaders, and learn how students are responding to the gospel. You'll hear student stories, meet young staff apprentices, hear from Kevin Young, and learn about Cru High School's three-year strategic initiatives, with opportunities for personal ministry involvement and to invest financially should the Lord so lead.

When

Thursday, June 24 to Sunday, June 27, 2027

Where

The Historic Stanley Hotel, Estes Park, Colorado. Opened in 1909, the hotel sits above the town of Estes Park with views into Rocky Mountain National Park.

Cost

Guest accommodations, breakfast, lunch, and dinners are provided at no cost. Guests cover their own airfare and transportation. Optional local excursions require a fee.

Free time and excursions

Afternoons in the Rockies

Afternoons are free time. Here are a few favorite ways to spend them on your own or with your family.

Rocky Mountain National Park

Just minutes from the hotel. Walk around an alpine lake, take a family hike, or drive Trail Ridge Road and watch for elk. The park may require a timed-entry reservation in summer, so check nps.gov before you go.

Downtown Estes Park

Stroll the Riverwalk, browse the shops on Elkhorn Avenue, and grab saltwater taffy or ice cream. Kids love it, and you might even spot elk wandering through town.

Horseback riding

Several stables around Estes Park lead guided trail rides for beginners and experienced riders, some into the national park. They're a short drive from the hotel, and booking ahead is a good idea.

Stanley Hotel history tour

The hotel offers guided tours most days. Hear the story of F.O. Stanley, how the hotel was built in 1909, and the legends that made it famous. Reserve through the hotel.
